Delete your account

Thiyal Desk (Business Management System) · app.paavaifashions.com

You can permanently delete your account yourself, from the app or the website, at any time.

How to delete

  1. Open the app (Android or iPhone) or go to app.paavaifashions.com and sign in.
  2. Open My Profile (on a phone: More → My Profile).
  3. Scroll to Delete Account, tap Delete my account, type DELETE and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately. Signing in with the account afterwards is not possible.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in, name, email address and phone number.
  • Any saved Face ID / fingerprint login on that device.
  • Your name is removed from the shop's history (it shows as "Deleted user").

If you are the only member of your shop

Deleting your account also permanently deletes the shop and everything in it — all orders, customers, payments, expenses and uploaded files. This cannot be undone.

If your shop has other team members

The shop and its records stay for the remaining team. If you are the shop's only Administrator, first make another member an Administrator (More → Users & Roles), then delete your account.

Can't sign in?

If you've lost access to your account, ask your shop's Administrator to switch you to Inactive in Users & Roles, which stops your access immediately. To have your account itself deleted, sign in and use the steps above.
