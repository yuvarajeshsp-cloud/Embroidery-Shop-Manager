Privacy Policy

Thiyal Desk (Business Management System) · app.paavaifashions.com · Last updated: 8 October 2026

Thiyal Desk (Business Management System) is an order, customer, payment and expense management app for embroidery and tailoring shops. This page explains what information the app handles and what happens to it.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider).
  • Business records you enter: customers, orders, items, payments, expenses, notes and your shop's settings and logo.
  • Files you upload: photos and documents you attach to orders, including pictures taken with your camera or chosen from your photo library when you ask the app to.
  • Activity records: an audit log of who changed what inside your shop, and the time.

Information stored on your device

  • Your sign-in session, so you stay signed in.
  • If you turn on Face ID / fingerprint sign-in, your email and password are saved in your phone's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). Fingerprint and face data are handled entirely by your phone's operating system; the app never receives or stores them.

How we use it

Only to run the app for your shop: signing you in, verifying your account, showing your data to you and the team members you authorise, and keeping a record of changes. We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising or tracking.

Who it is shared with

We use these service providers to operate the app. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and file storage (hosted in Singapore).
  • Twilio — sends the verification code to your phone number by SMS when you sign up.
  • Netlify — hosts the web version of the app.

Each shop's data is separated from every other shop's; people in other shops cannot see your records.

Who can see your data inside a shop

Your shop's Administrator controls who joins your shop and what role they have. Team members can see the records their role allows.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from My Profile → Delete Account in the app, or follow the steps on our account deletion page. Deleting your account removes your personal details. If you are the only member of a shop, the shop and all its records and files are deleted too.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is restricted by role and shop, and passwords are never stored in readable form. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Children

The app is for business use and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: sign in at app.paavaifashions.com and contact your shop's Administrator, or use Delete Account in My Profile to remove your data yourself.
